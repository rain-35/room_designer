Quick tutorial (5 minutes)

Plan one room from scratch. Everything is drawn to scale: 1 square of the grid is 12 inches (1 foot) by default.

  1. Set the room size. In the top bar type a Width and Length, for example 12' and 14'. You can also type 12' 6", 150 (inches) or 381 cm.
  2. Add furniture. In the left panel, click an item (try Queen bed) to drop it in the room, or drag it onto the plan. Use the search box to find things.
  3. Move it. Drag the piece. It snaps to the grid, walls and other pieces. Pink guide lines show what it is lined up with. Hold Alt to move freely.
  4. Turn and resize it. With the piece selected, press R to rotate. Drag a corner handle to resize, or type exact sizes in the right panel.
  5. Add a door and a window. Under Doors & windows in the library, click Door. Slide it along the wall by dragging. Set which way it swings in the right panel.
  6. Check the fit. Red pieces overlap. Dashed lines mark walkways that are too tight. The numbers shown from a selected piece to each wall are the gaps.
  7. Print it. Click Print / PNG, pick a paper size and scale such as 1/4" = 1', then print or save an image.

Your work saves automatically. See Layouts & syncing for how it reaches your other computers.

Moving around the plan

  • Zoom: mouse wheel, or the + / − buttons.
  • Pan: hold the middle mouse button and drag, or hold Space and drag.
  • Fit shows the current room. House shows every room.

Rooms

Size and shape

  • A new room is a rectangle. Type its Width and Length in the top bar or the right panel.
  • L-shape… (right panel) asks for the overall size and the notch to cut from one corner. Doors and windows on that room are removed because the walls change; add them back afterward.
  • Edit corners turns the room into a shape with movable walls and corners (see below). Type exact wall lengths in the Walls list.
  • Make rectangle turns it back into a plain rectangle of the same overall size.
  • Walls cannot cross each other and cannot be shorter than 6 inches.

Moving walls and corners (odd shapes like an L or T)

  • Drag a wall to push the whole wall straight in or out. The walls beside it get longer or shorter. Doors and windows on it move with it. The cursor changes to a resize arrow when you are over a wall.
  • Drag a corner to move just that corner.
  • Both snap to grid lines and to the lines of other corners, in whole inches. Hold Alt for free movement.
  • Keep corners square (90°) is a checkbox in the room's properties, on by default for L-shapes and new "Edit corners" rooms. While it is on, every wall stays horizontal or vertical, so all corners are right angles, and you can still shape the room into an L, T, U and so on. Dragging a corner slides its two neighboring walls along with it.
  • Making an L: click the + in the middle of a wall to add a point on it, then drag that point into the room (or out). The point becomes the inside corner of an L, and the nearer end of that wall is cut back (or pushed out) to meet it. The room keeps its 90° corners the whole time. Doors and windows stay where they are, but any that fall in the cut-away part are removed.
  • Shift+click the + adds a small step instead (24" wide, 12" deep) that you drag in or out, handy for a T or an alcove.
  • Typing a wall length moves the next wall, so corners stay square.
  • Removing a corner (select it, then press Delete or click Remove corner): with square corners on, the room snaps back to the walls beyond it. For example, removing the inside corner of an L fills the notch and gives a rectangle again. Doors and windows in the filled-in part are removed. If the removal would make walls cross, you are told, and you can untick the box to remove it freely.
  • If you untick the box and bend walls out of square, ticking it again straightens them, or tells you if they are too far off.
  • With the box unticked you can drag corners anywhere, click + to add a corner, and select a corner and press Delete to remove it.

Several rooms in one layout

  • Open the Rooms list at the top of the right panel. Add room puts a new room beside the others; Duplicate copies the current one; Delete room removes it (a layout always keeps at least one room).
  • Click a room on the plan, or in the list, to work on it. Width, Length, shape buttons and furniture all apply to that room.
  • Move a room: drag its name tag, or type Left edge and Top edge in the right panel. Rooms snap to each other's walls.
  • If a door sits on a wall that touches another room, the neighbor's wall opens too, forming a shared doorway.
  • With more than one room the top bar shows this room's area and the house total.

Furniture

  • Add: click an item in the library or drag it onto a room. A piece dropped in a room goes in that room.
  • Select: click a piece. The bar on the canvas offers Rotate, Duplicate and Delete.
  • Resize: drag a corner handle, or type Width and Depth in the right panel. Circles have one size, the diameter.
  • Rotate: R turns 45° clockwise, Shift+R counter-clockwise, or pick an angle in the right panel.
  • Color: each category has a color. Change one piece's color in the right panel; Category color resets it.
  • Locked keeps a piece from being moved or deleted by accident. Ignore clearance checks stops it from causing walkway warnings (handy for wall art or a rug).
  • Rugs and other floor items draw underneath, so furniture on top of them is not flagged as overlapping.

Your own pieces and categories

Click New piece to save a piece with its own name, size, shape and category, or New category for a new group and color. Use the ✎ and × buttons beside your own pieces to edit or delete them. Your library is saved with your account, so it follows you to other computers.

Doors & windows

  • Add one from Doors & windows in the library: Door, narrow Door, Window, wide Window, or Open doorway.
  • Slide it along a wall by dragging. Drag it to another wall to move it there. Click it and use the arrow keys to nudge (Shift for 6 inches).
  • In the right panel choose its Wall, its distance from the wall's start, its Width, and for a door whether it opens into or out of the room and which side the hinge is on (seen from inside the room, facing the wall).
  • The shaded quarter circle is the door swing. It turns red when furniture is in the way.
  • The Walls list for corner-edited rooms numbers walls 1, 2, 3… in order around the room.

Snapping & sizes

  • Grid sets the grid squares: 1", 6" or 12".
  • Snap (on by default) makes pieces, doors, windows, room corners and rooms jump to nearby grid lines, walls and other pieces. It only grabs when you are close.
  • Whole inches: when you drag to resize a piece or move a room corner, sizes change in whole inches, not fractions. Resized pieces also snap to whole grid squares when near.
  • Hold Alt while dragging to turn all of this off and get free, fractional sizes.
  • Numbers you type are never rounded. Fractions work: 19 5/8".
  • Units switches the display between feet/inches and metric. Your data is not changed.

Warnings

Turn Warnings on or off in the top bar. When on, the layout is checked for:

  • Overlaps: pieces that overlap turn red.
  • Tight walkways: a gap of 6 inches up to the minimum between two pieces, or a piece and a wall, gets a dashed line and a size. Set the minimum under Min walkway in the room's properties (default 36 inches).
  • Blocked doors: a door whose swing is hit by furniture turns red.

Gaps smaller than 6 inches are treated as a piece pushed against a wall and are not flagged.

Measuring

Click Measure, then click two points. The distance appears on the plan, and it can span different rooms. Press Esc to clear it, and again to leave the tool. Snap helps you land exactly on corners and grid lines.

When you select a piece, the distances from it to the walls around it are also shown automatically.

Layouts & syncing

  • The Layouts button lists all your layouts. Open, rename or delete them, or start a new one. To compare two arrangements, Export the layout and Import it again; the import opens as a separate copy.
  • The layout name is the box in the top bar.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover everything: moves, resizes, new rooms, and so on. A drag counts as one step.
  • Autosave: changes are saved on this computer straight away and sent to the cloud when you are online. The text beside Sync shows the state. Click Sync to force it.
  • Offline: you can keep working without internet. Changes upload the next time you are online.
  • Conflicts: if the same layout was changed on two computers, you are asked what to do: keep this computer's copy, keep the cloud copy, keep both (one becomes a new layout), or decide later.
  • Sign out removes the saved copies from this computer. If something has not been uploaded yet you will be warned first.
Use Export now and then to keep a JSON backup file of a layout.

Print & export

Print / PNG

  1. Click Print / PNG.
  2. Pick a Paper size and a Scale. Best fit picks the largest standard scale that fits, such as 1/4" = 1'.
  3. Choose what to show: room names and areas, wall lengths, furniture names, furniture sizes.
  4. Save PNG downloads an image. Print / PDF opens your browser's print window; choose Save as PDF as the printer for a PDF file.
Print at 100% (not "fit to page") so the scale is true. Measure a wall on the paper against the scale bar to check.

The plan has to fit on one page. If it does not, choose a bigger paper size or a smaller scale. The preview shows a warning in that case.

Export / Import (JSON)

  • Export saves the open layout as a file you can keep or share.
  • Import reads such a file. By default it opens as a new layout; you can choose to replace an existing one, after a confirmation.

Troubleshooting

I cannot sign in or create an account

Sign-ups are turned off on purpose; the owner's account is made by hand. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to get a reset link by email.

Changes do not show up on my other computer

Click Sync on both computers and look at the status beside it. If it says offline, check the internet connection.

I cannot drag a piece

It may be Locked; uncheck that in the right panel. Or the Measure tool is on; press Esc.

A piece will not snap where I want, or sizes jump

Hold Alt while dragging, or turn Snap off. You can always type an exact value instead.

My L-shape or corner move was refused

Walls cannot cross, and each must be at least 6 inches long.

The printout is the wrong size

Make sure the print window is set to 100% / "Actual size", and the paper size matches the one chosen in the Print / PNG window.

The page looks out of date after an update

Reload with Ctrl+Shift+R to clear the browser's saved copy.
